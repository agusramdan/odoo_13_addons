## Compatibility and route contract

After transfer, installations with only `amr_token` will no longer expose `/oidc/token`. To receive the endpoint, installation must include `amr_oidc`, which depends on `amr_token`. There is no dual-route compatibility mechanism in this proposal. Other OIDC routes, including JWKS, introspection, profile, authorization, and discovery, are not part of this transfer; they remain unchanged and are owned/deferred as assigned to ADDONS13-38.
